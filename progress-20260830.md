## Session: mimic-completeness gate (tests/test_mimic.py)

Alan requested the gate after spotting two mimic defects in a row. Design
follows the "close the class, not the instance" rule:

- **Coverage is derived, not enumerated**: every top-level plant-config
  section, every `network.cv_*` key, and every actuator must map to a drawn
  symbol/label (`text_of`/`class_of`) or appear in `WAIVED` with a reason —
  so a future hotwell release adding hardware fails the panel gate until the
  drawing catches up. 12 required symbols present, 6 waived (design, firing,
  actuators.firing, audit, control, pumps→own test).
- **Pump symbols** must equal `PUMP_NAMES` exactly (missing AND stale both
  fail).
- **`data-*` hooks** (17 on the board) must name live contract keys:
  `data-pump` ∈ PUMP_NAMES, `data-indication` ∈ Observation.indications,
  `data-flow` ∈ OBSERVATION_SPEC.
- **Connectivity is geometric**: a strict M/L/H/V/Z path parser (unknown
  commands fail loudly rather than escaping the check); every
  flow-line/pipe/shaft endpoint (34 on the current board) must land within
  2 px of a symbol bbox, another line's segment, or within a 16×10 px box of
  a labeled terminal (BUS). This is the floating-condenser bug generalized.

**Gate proven able to fail** (the §11.3 lesson): run against the pre-fix
board from `git show 184bdaf:client/src/board.svg` it reports `LCV` absent
and exactly one dangling endpoint — `flow-line (852.0, 222.0)`, the exact
condenser defect. Verbatim: `dangling endpoints on old board: [(['flow-line'],
(852.0, 222.0))]`.

Verification on current board: `4 passed in 0.22s` (prints: 4 pumps, 17
hooks, 12+6 coverage, 34 endpoints 0 dangling); full suite `99 passed in
89.75s`; ruff clean after a `zip(..., strict=False)` annotation.
